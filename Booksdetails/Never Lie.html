Psychological Thriller, Mystery, Contemporary Fiction

Never Lie

Helena Court

Book Description :

Never Lie is a suspenseful psychological thriller that delves into the intricate world of deception and its far-reaching consequences.
The story follows a central character who becomes entangled in a web of lies that gradually unravels the truth about those closest to them.
As secrets come to light and hidden agendas are revealed, the narrative probes into themes of trust, betrayal, and the moral dilemmas that arise when the truth is obscured. With its atmospheric tension and unpredictable twists, the novel challenges readers to consider whether a single lie can disrupt and redefine the course of an entire life.
