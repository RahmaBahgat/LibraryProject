Fourth Wing

Rebecca Yarros

Book Description :

Fourth Wing is the first book in The Empyrean series.
The story follows Violet Sorrengail, a young woman forced to join the brutal war college for dragon riders, Basgiath War College.
Although she was meant to live a quiet life among scholars, her commanding mother demands she train to become a dragon rider—a deadly path where cadets die trying.
With enemies both on the battlefield and within the academy, and dragons who will only bond with the worthy, Violet must fight to survive in a world where weakness is a death sentence.
As secrets unravel and tensions rise, Violet discovers that the greatest danger may not be the battlefield—but the people she thought she could trust.
