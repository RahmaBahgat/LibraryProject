Rebecca Yarros

Rebecca Yarros is a USA Today and New York Times bestselling author who combines swoon-worthy romance with heart-pounding adventure? A mom of six and a military wife, she brings emotional depth and fierce heroines to life in her books.

Born: 1980s, United States
