Frequently Asked Questions

How do I borrow a book?

To borrow a book, you need to:

  1. Create an account or log in
  2. Browse our collection and find a book you like
  3. Click the "Borrow" button on the book's page
  4. Confirm your borrowing request

How long can I keep a borrowed book?

Books can be borrowed for up to 14 days. You can renew your borrowing period if no one else has requested the book.

How do I return a book?

To return a book:

  1. Go to your profile page
  2. Find the book in your borrowed books list
  3. Click the "Return" button
