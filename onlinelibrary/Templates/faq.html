Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about InOtherWords Library

1. Account & Registration

How do I create an account?

You can create an account by clicking the "Sign Up" button and filling out the registration form with your email and password.

Is membership free?

Yes, creating an account and using the basic features of InOtherWords is completely free.

Can I change my password?

Yes, you can change your password through your profile settings after logging in.

2. Borrowing Books

How many books can I borrow?

You can borrow up to 5 books at a time. You'll need to return some books before borrowing more.

What is the loan period?

The standard loan period is 14 days. You may be able to renew if there are no holds on the book.

Are there late fees?

We have a 3-day grace period. After that, your account may have borrowing restrictions until books are returned.

3. Technical Support

How do I report issues?

You can report technical issues through our Help Center or by contacting support directly.

Browser compatibility?

Our website works best with modern browsers like Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.

Mobile access?

Yes, our website is fully responsive and can be accessed on any mobile device or tablet.
